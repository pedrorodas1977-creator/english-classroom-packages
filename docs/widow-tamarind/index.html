Short Adapted Reading / Lectura adaptada corta

At night, a beautiful woman appears near a tamarind tree in Posorja, Guayas. Some people say she looks elegant and quiet. A man sees her after a long night and follows her because he thinks she is beautiful. The street is dark, and the tree is alone. When the man gets closer, the woman changes. Her face is not beautiful now. She looks like a skeleton with a woman's clothes. The man feels afraid and runs away. People in the community tell this legend to warn others and to remember local stories. Some people believe the story, and some people do not. In class, we listen with respect. We can say, "I believe," "I don't believe," or "I think it is scary."
